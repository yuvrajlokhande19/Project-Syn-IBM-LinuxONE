# 📚 Academic Research & Technical Foundation

Project Syn bridges enterprise hardware architecture, zero-trust cryptography, and deterministic graph optimization. Below are the foundational peer-reviewed papers, engineering standards, and technical specifications underpinning the system:

---

### 1. IBM Mainframe Silicon, Cryptography & Hardware Acceleration
1. **IBM z16 Technical Guide** (2022). IBM Redbooks (SG24-8950).  
   *Focus: Central Processor Assist for Cryptographic Function (CPACF) with MSA extensions (`KMAC`, `KIMD`), Pervasive Encryption, and on-chip low-latency cryptographic coprocessors.*
2. **Duan, H., et al.** (2023). "Accelerated Machine Learning Inference on IBM Z and LinuxONE Systems." *IBM Journal of Research and Development*, 67(4), pp. 1-12.  
   *Focus: Native on-chip AI acceleration via the Telum Neural Network Processing Assist (`nnpa`) instruction set.*
3. **Brunschwiler, T., et al.** (2021). "Snap Machine Learning: Fast Training and Inference for Generalized Linear Models." *Proceedings of the 27th ACM SIGKDD International Conference on Knowledge Discovery & Data Mining*, pp. 3788-3796.  
   *Focus: Extreme-performance gradient boosted trees and Random Forests optimized for cache hierarchies on enterprise systems.*

---

### 2. Urban Resilience, Mass Casualty Triage & Graph Optimization
4. **Kermanshah, A., & Derrible, S.** (2017). "Robustness of road systems to extreme flooding using network science." *Computer-Aided Civil and Infrastructure Engineering*, 32(9), 707-722.  
   *Focus: Dynamic graph reweighting under pluvial urban flooding and isolating critical access bridges during storm surges.*
5. **Dijkstra, E. W.** (1959). "A note on two problems in connexion with graphs." *Numerische Mathematik*, 1(1), 269-271.  
   *Focus: Mathematical foundations for deterministic single-source shortest path calculation with zero stochastic variance for safety-critical emergency ambulance corridors.*
6. **National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), Govt. of India** (2021). *National Disaster Management Guidelines on Hospital Safety and Mass Casualty Transport During Urban Cloudburst Events*. New Delhi.  
   *Focus: Protocols for inter-hospital resource leveling (blood components, cryogenic liquid oxygen, ICU beds) across primary urban apex centers.*
